Behaviour
Filters: one place (select) AND any of the chosen scopes (OR within scope). Sort: place A–Z or type A–Z, never by date. State lives in the URL: ?place=tharad&scope=cnc,fero&sort=type.
Desktop: the whole row is one link to /projects/[slug]. Mobile: tapping a row expands it; "Open project" goes to the page.
Scope marks, in order: Design · Hand carving · CNC · Construction · Fero; the legend sits above the table. Filled navy = in scope; outlined slate = not (3.11:1 on sand, non-text UI ≥ 3:1). The full words are in aria-label.
MOTION · Flowing Menu
Trigger: hover or focus (desktop). The navy band slides in from the edge the pointer entered (top or bottom), 0.6 s expo.out; the marquee loops at 40 px/s, linear; exit reverses in 0.4 s. Row height stays fixed (no CLS).
Mobile expand: GSAP height 0 → auto, 0.45 s power2.out; arch photo opens from the bottom, 0.6 s.
Reduced motion: band appears in place with no slide or loop; expand is instant.
Mobile filter sheet: opens from the bottom, 0.35 s power3.out; focus is trapped; Esc and Done close it.
