Poster frames

Each frame is a finished image: it is the low-end, no-WebGL2, Save-Data and reduced-motion fallback, and the first paint before the 3D chunk loads. The 3D scene must match the framing, light direction and grade shown here. The relief and mountain shown are art-direction stand-ins built from simple shapes. [A2] The final poster is rendered from the real scene (the chosen CNC panel, and the modelled mountain) with the same camera.

P3

Fero Works: the fly-through

This replaces the parting mountain. The section is pinned for 500vh on desktop and 400vh on mobile. Scroll progress 0 → 1 drives one camera along a spline through five shots. Scrub is smoothed with scrub: 0.6. Every shot is also a finished poster; the HTML text (dashed outlines) sits on top of the poster and is never baked into the image. Text is only ever placed over night, navy or rock, never over saffron glow, cloud rims or rays. Palette: night, navy, slate, slate-deep, saffron, saffron-text, sand, stone and sky only.

Scene and camera rig
1 unit = 1 m. Cloud deck top at 420 m; ridges 60–110 m; mountain 140 m; cave mouth 36 × 44 m; temple 24 m including the flag, set 40 m inside the mouth.
Camera position and lookAt each follow a CatmullRomCurve3 through five nodes, sampled by the same eased progress. Base fov 38°.
Camera nodes (pos · target)
S1 (0, 520, 900) · (0, 440, −1200)
S2 (0, 360, 520) · (0, 120, −600)
S3 (−60, 70, 0) · (40, 50, −500)
S4 (70, 55, −520) · (0, 40, −900)
S5 (0, 22, −840) · (0, 18, −960), fov 30°
Mobile
Same nodes. The vertical fov is the desktop value × 1.23, so the 9:16 crop matches the frames below (centre 39% of the width, full height). The temple, the cave and every text-safe area sit inside that crop.
Fallbacks
Reduced motion: no pin, no canvas. The five posters stack as full-bleed 100svh frames, each with its own HTML text state as shown. No crossfades.
Low-end (no WebGL2, Save-Data, deviceMemory ≤ 4): pinned for 400vh, the five posters stacked, each crossfading over 0.06 of progress at its boundary. No 3D.
First paint: S4 until the chunk loads.

Nagara shikhara · model spec
Frontal elevation, symmetric. Proportions: shikhara 0.48 of the total height, wall 0.18, jagati 0.12.
Tiers (bhumi) every 2.3% of the height, as recessed bands. The central ratha is lighter and projects 0.3 m. Each urushringa is a scaled copy (0.45× and 0.28×) with its own amalaka and kalash.
Material: white stone (sand to stone). Kalash and flag are saffron. Lighting is a warm key from the cave floor plus a saffron rim on both edges; the sanctum door is emissive.
