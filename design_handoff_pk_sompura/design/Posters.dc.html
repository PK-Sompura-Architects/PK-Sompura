Poster frames

Each frame is a finished image: it is the low-end, no-WebGL2, Save-Data and reduced-motion fallback, and the first paint before the 3D chunk loads. The 3D scene must match the framing, light direction and grade shown here. The relief and mountain shown are art-direction stand-ins built from simple shapes. [A2] The final poster is rendered from the real scene (the chosen CNC panel, and the modelled mountain) with the same camera.

Light
Key light: low raking light from upper-left, elevation 12°, azimuth 30° at rest. Shadows fall to lower right. Warm key (sand), cool fill (navy at 35%).
Grade: highlights clip to sand #F4EFE6, deepest shadow no darker than navy #262654 at 45% over stone.
Motion · 3D scene 1 (three.js)
Trigger: scroll (plus cursor x on desktop pointer:fine). Light azimuth 30°→70° across the hero's scroll range; cursor adds ±10°. Lerp 0.08 per frame, no easing curve (damped follow).
Poster → canvas crossfade 600 ms, ease-out, after first frame renders.

P3

Fero Works: the mountain that opens

Section pinned for 250vh. Scroll progress 0 → 1 maps to the four keyframes. Each keyframe is exported as its own poster; K3 is the one shown when 3D does not run. Topography contours sit behind (slate #798A96 at 22%), Light Rays in saffron come from inside the mountain, Noise grain over everything, Gradual Blur on top and bottom edges.

K1 Closed. Mountain whole. A thin saffron seam shows light inside. Topography only, no rays. Enter: page ground crossfades sand → night over 40vh before pin (scrub, ease none).
K2 Opening. Halves part 7% each side. Rays fade in to 45%. Temple visible, dim. Scrubbed, power2.inOut mapped on progress 0→0.35.
K3 Open. Halves at 29%. White-stone shikhara lit from inside in saffron; rays full. Hold for 0.70→0.85 (no movement: a rest the eye can read). This is the reduced-motion and low-end poster.
K4 Transition. Rays ease to 60%; plate 01 (Sketch) of the Scroll Stack rises over the scene; Gradual Blur band above it. Pin releases, canvas stops rendering once off-screen.
