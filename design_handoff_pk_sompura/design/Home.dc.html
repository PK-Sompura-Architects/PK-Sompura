Homepage, section by section

Each row is one section: spec and motion note · desktop 1440 · mobile 360. Every frame is the static state, so it reads the same with motion switched off. The grey-beige board around the frames is presentation only and never ships.

Hero: the carved panel
Layout: 6 cols text / 6 cols + right bleed poster. Mobile: text, CTAs, full-bleed 4:5 poster.
Components: Nav, Display heading (cut), Button primary + text link, PosterFrame.
MOTION
3D scene 1 (three.js, not React Bits). Trigger: scroll + cursor. Light azimuth 30°→70°, damped lerp 0.08. Loads on idle after LCP; poster→canvas crossfade 600 ms ease-out.

Gradual Blur: bottom 96 px of poster while pinned.
Reduced motion: poster only, no pin.

The family
Layout: label cols 1–3, paragraph cols 4–12. Statement type (Caslon 52 / mobile 30).

Trigger: scroll, scrubbed from paragraph top at 80% viewport to bottom at 35%. Word colour deep slate → navy, ease none (scrub), no blur, no rotation.

CNC Works
Layout: heading cols 1–7, text cols 9–12; toolpath poster 12 cols; three portrait photos, 4 cols each. Mobile: poster full-bleed, photos in a scroll-snap row (the row scrolls, the page does not).

Line Waves

§6 · Night theme
Fero Works: the mountain
Layout: heading + text; pinned full-bleed mountain (16:9 / mobile 9:14); build sequence heading; five plates, 5 cols text + 7 cols image.
Components: ChapterMarker, Heading (night), PosterFrame, StackPlate, PhotoFrame (night).
MOTION
Theme: sand → night crossfade over 40vh, scrubbed.
Mountain (3D scene 2): pinned 250vh, K1→K4 on scroll. Light Rays saffron inside; Topography behind (drift 0.1); Noise 5%; Gradual Blur top/bottom 12%.
Scroll Stack: each plate pins at top 120 px; previous plate scales to 0.96 and dims to 80%, 1 plate per 70vh, Lenis smoothing 1.1. Plates are a plain list in HTML.
Reduced motion: K3 poster, no pin; plates as the plain list shown here.
